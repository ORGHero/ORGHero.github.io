HERO MEDIA PLAYER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

HERO Media Player ("HERO", "the app") is developed by ORGHero. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Your media stays on your device

HERO plays video and audio files that are already on your device or that you open yourself. Your files, playlists, watch history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any server that receives them.

2. Network streams you add

If you open a network stream or add an IPTV / M3U playlist URL, the app connects directly to the address you entered. HERO does not provide, host or sell any channels or content. You are responsible for having the right to watch what you open.

3. AI subtitles

The automatic subtitle feature runs completely on your device. Audio is not uploaded anywhere.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and basic device information. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Advanced → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. HERO Pro purchase

HERO Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether HERO Pro is owned, to turn ads off.

6. Logs

The app keeps a technical log on your device to help fix playback problems. It is never sent automatically; it leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

7. Children

HERO is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from Google AdMob and Google Play as described above, no data is shared with third parties.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
